## Expected behavior

An artifact is successful only after the migrated configuration and its credentials are durably verified.

## Recommended implementation

1. Handle configuration/credential destination errors explicitly and record success only after durable destination validation.
2. Use a destination-aware migration revision contract instead of applying an unrelated legacy revision through normal optimistic concurrency. Receipt entries must distinguish failed, skipped and validated outcomes.
