Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Omera.

Does Omera upload any of my images, prompts, or personal data to the cloud? ▼

No, absolutely not. Omera is 100% local-first and works completely offline. All image scanning, prompt inspection, thumbnail caching, and indexing stay strictly on your local machine with zero telemetry or cloud sync.

Can I drag images directly from Omera into ComfyUI or WebUI? ▼

Yes! Native OS drag-and-drop is fully supported. Simply drag any artwork card from Omera directly onto your ComfyUI canvas to instantly reconstruct the full workflow node graph, or into WebUI PNG Info to reload all generation parameters.

Will my computer lag if I have over 50,000+ high-resolution AI images in a folder? ▼

Not at all. Omera uses an ultra-fast virtual gallery that only renders what is currently visible on screen, coupled with intelligent background thumbnail processing. Your interface stays buttery smooth with low RAM and CPU usage, even with 100,000+ images.

Is Omera completely free and open source? ▼

Yes! Omera is 100% free and open source under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0 (AGPL-3.0). There are no paywalls, subscriptions, or hidden limits.
